# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and contributors of different experience levels.
- Respect maintainers' decisions made to keep the project safe and productive.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, doxxing, spam, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting
Contact the repository owner or a maintainer privately through GitHub with relevant context. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
